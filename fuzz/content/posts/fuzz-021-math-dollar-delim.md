## Inline Math with $...$

Pythagorean: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$.

Euler: $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$.

Quadratic: $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$.

## Block Math with $$...$$

$$
\int_{0}^{\infty} e^{-x^2} dx = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}
$$

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6}
$$
