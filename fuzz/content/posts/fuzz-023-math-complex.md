## Complex Expressions

### Fractions

$$
\frac{\frac{a}{b}}{\frac{c}{d}} = \frac{ad}{bc}
$$

### Sums

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^k}{k!} = e^x
$$

### Limits

$$
\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1
$$

### Integrals

$$
\iint_D f(x,y) \, dx \, dy
$$

$$
\oint_C \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r}
$$
